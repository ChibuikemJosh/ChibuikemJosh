## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of Lagos, Nigeria, interested in building reliable software systems and applying technology to real-world problems.

My work spans backend engineering, AI integration, financial technology, and data-driven applications. I enjoy understanding how systems work under the hood, making thoughtful architectural decisions, and turning ideas into working software.

* 🔭 **Currently building:** [MarketPulse](https://github.com/ChibuikemJosh/MarketPulse) and developing the Nexa AI concept.
* ⚙️ **Engineering focus:** Backend architecture, API development, databases, caching, and scalable applications.
* 🤖 **AI interests:** AI orchestration, LLM integration, machine learning, and intelligent systems.
* 📈 **Fintech interests:** Financial data, stock analysis, quantitative methods, and market-data applications.
* 🌱 **Currently exploring:** Distributed systems, software architecture, and applied machine learning.
* 🤝 **Open to:** Interesting engineering projects, open-source contributions, and technical collaborations.
